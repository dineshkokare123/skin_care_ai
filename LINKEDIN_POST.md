🚀 **Excited to share my latest project: SkinCare AI — Your Personal Assistant for Glowing Skin!** ✨

I’ve been building a comprehensive, AI-powered mobile application that brings dermatology-grade advice right to your pocket. Built with **Flutter** and powered by **Google's Gemini API**, this app bridges the gap between technology and self-care.

📱 **Key Features:**

* **🤖 AI Skin Analysis:** A smart quiz that identifies your skin type and concerns (Acne, Aging, sensitivity) to generate a personalized routine in seconds.
* **💬 AI Dermatologist Chat:** Have a question? The integrated AI consultant provides instant, science-backed skincare advice.
* **🧴 Curated Marketplace:** A seamless shopping experience with product details, reviews, and a simulated secure checkout.
* **📊 Seller Analytics:** A powerful dashboard for sellers to track revenue trends and top-performing products using interactive charts.
* **💎 Premium UI/UX:** Designed with a "Liquid Glass" aesthetic, featuring soft glassmorphism, smooth animations, and a calming sage-green theme.

🛠 **Tech Stack:**

* **Framework:** Flutter & Dart 🎯
* **AI:** Google Gemini API 🧠
* **State Management:** Riverpod 🌊
* **Navigation:** GoRouter 🧭
* **Analytics:** FL Chart 📈

This project was a fantastic journey into integrating Generative AI into a real-world mobile experience, focusing heavily on performance and premium design.
